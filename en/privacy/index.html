2. Information collected

The contact form collects your name, email address, message, and selected language. To prevent abuse, it also processes a Cloudflare-provided country code, the Turnstile verification result, and salted hashes derived from the IP address and email address.

3. Purpose of use

The information is used only to review and respond to inquiries and to operate the form safely, including preventing abuse, spam, and repeated submissions.

4. Abuse and spam prevention

IP and email addresses are hashed for rate limiting; their raw values are not stored in the site's own storage. Content classification only adds tags to the received email subject. Messages are not discarded solely because of classification.

5. Disclosure to third parties

Personal information is not disclosed to third parties except where required by law. Service providers may process information as necessary for email delivery and receipt, analytics, and abuse prevention through the external services described below.

6. External services

This site uses Cloudflare Workers and Email Service, embedded YouTube videos, and Google Analytics. When you view the site or play a video, information may be processed under each provider's terms and privacy policy.

7. Turnstile verification

The contact form uses Cloudflare Turnstile. To verify that a submission is not automated, Turnstile sends browser and connection-related information to Cloudflare for verification.

8. Email delivery and receipt

Cloudflare Email Service is used to deliver contact messages. Gmail is used to receive and reply to them. The email address entered in the form is used as the Reply-To address.

10. Access, correction, and deletion

To request access, correction, or deletion of contact information, use the Contact page. Identity verification may be required.

11. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated in response to changes in services or applicable law. Material changes will be announced on this page.
